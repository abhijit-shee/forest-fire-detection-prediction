How the system works

From a raw sensor reading to a coordinated field response.

ForestGuard AI keeps the detection chain explicit at every step, so an operator can always see which measurement triggered which alert — and why.

01

Sense

Devices report temperature, smoke, humidity and battery to an authenticated ingestion endpoint.

02

Evaluate

Every reading is scored against configurable thresholds with persistence and rate-of-change checks.

03

Alert

Explained alerts are created once per condition, deduplicated, and escalated as severity grows.

04

Respond

Officers acknowledge, investigate, log notes and resolve — with a full timestamped timeline.

Platform

Built for monitoring rooms, field teams and research.

Every module works end to end — dashboards, device registry, alert centre, incident management and reporting share one data model.

Sensor Network Monitoring

Track temperature, smoke, humidity and connectivity across every registered device in your forest zones, with a full reading history.

Rule-Based Fire Detection

Transparent, configurable rules convert raw readings into explained warnings — never an unexplained black-box score.

Geospatial Incident Mapping

See zone boundaries, device positions and incident markers on one live map, filterable by risk, status and zone.

Escalating Alerts

Critical alerts notify subscribed users, wait for acknowledgement, then escalate to designated officers on deadline.

Simulation Training Mode

Run a seven-stage guided fire scenario to demonstrate and validate response workflows without any physical hardware.

Role-Based Access

Administrators, forest officers and general users each see exactly the data and controls their role permits.

Environmental impact

Earlier awareness gives responders a real window to act.

Forest fires spread fastest in the first hours. A monitoring system earns its value by shortening the gap between an abnormal reading and a human deciding what to do about it — not by claiming outcomes it cannot prove.

Faster triage

Every alert carries its triggering measurements so officers can judge urgency without re-reading raw data.

Wildlife protection

Zone-level risk visibility helps prioritise patrols in habitats that are hardest to reach.

Fire-weather awareness

Temperature, humidity and smoke trends together reveal drying conditions before ignition.

Accountable response

Timestamped acknowledgements and notes create an auditable record of every incident.

System limitations and responsible use

Prototype, not certification. ForestGuard AI is a demonstration platform. It does not replace official emergency services, certified fire-detection systems, or trained human judgement.

Estimates, not guarantees. Risk scores are rule-based estimates from sensor readings. Threshold values shown are initial demonstration settings — not universally validated forest-fire thresholds.

Detection is not confirmation. An alert means measurements are unusual. A fire is only treated as verified after a responder confirms it in the field.

Connectivity matters. Notifications require network connectivity and may be delayed or unavailable. Do not rely on this system as the sole warning mechanism.
